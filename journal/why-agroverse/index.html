01 · The founder

Why I started Agroverse

A farmer's son, a master's in Germany, and a motorbike that kept going back to Araku.

10 November 2026 · 3 min read

My father, Yanala Sivaram Prasad, is a farmer in Andhra Pradesh. I grew up knowing that the person who grows the food is almost never the person who names the price.

I left for Germany and studied industrial engineering and international management at Hochschule Fresenius. I learned how supply chains are drawn on paper: fast, tidy, and far from the field.

Then I started riding to Araku Valley. I went on my own Royal Enfield, more than once, through monsoon roads and long nights at truck stops, to sit with the farmers and see the slopes myself. I did not want to buy coffee from a list. I wanted to know whose hands it came from.

Agroverse UG is built on that. A short, traceable line from named farmers in Araku to a cup in Berlin, with the farmers paid well and on time. Coffee is where it starts. The model is meant to travel to other origins and other crops.

The Pearl Coffees is the first brand. Everything else in this Journal is how we try to keep that promise in practice.
