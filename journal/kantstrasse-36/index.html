04 · The base

Our café at Kantstraße 36

Where the coffee meets Berlin.

10 November 2026 · 2 min read

Our base in Berlin is a café at Kantstraße 36. It opened on 19 October 2026.

A coffee brand from Araku needs a place where people can taste it, ask about it, and see that the story matches the cup. This is that place. Indian specialty coffee, made by people who know where it comes from.

It is also how the farmers' coffee reaches people who have never heard of Araku. Every cup poured here is one more reason for a café, a hotel or an office to try the same coffee.

Come by. Ask where it is from. We will tell you the village.
