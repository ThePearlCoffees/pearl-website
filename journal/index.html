Journal

Notes from Araku Valley.

The place

Why Araku Valley

Araku Valley lies in the Eastern Ghats of Andhra Pradesh, in the east of India. The coffee grows on highland slopes between 900 and 1,500 metres, in the shade of the forest.

It is a region of tribal farming communities. We chose it because the people and the place are the story: nothing in the cup is borrowed from somewhere else.

The people

57 farmers, six mandals

57 tribal farmers are onboarded in Araku Valley: 35 in Araku Valley, 8 in Ananthagiri, 5 in Hukumpeta, and 3 each in Pedabayalu, Manchingiput and Dumbriguda.

We work with them directly. There is an MOU with the Araku Tribal Development and Sustainable Agriculture Society, and the bag names where its coffee comes from.

The roast

What roasted at origin means

Most specialty coffee travels as green beans and is roasted in the country where it is sold. Araku Pearl is roasted in India, close to where it grew.

The roasted coffee is then shipped to Berlin and packed in 250 g bags. Forest Whisper is a natural process, medium dark roast, with notes of honey, sugarcane, chocolate and caramel.
