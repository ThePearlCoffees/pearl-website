03 · The route

From Araku to Berlin: the route of one bag

Harvest, roast at origin, courier, customs, and a German label.

10 November 2026 · 3 min read

Forest Whisper grows at about 1,150 metres. The wider Araku Valley runs from 900 to 1,500 metres. The harvest runs from November to March, under the forest canopy.

The cherries are processed and the coffee is roasted in Araku, not in Europe. Forest Whisper is a natural process, medium dark roast, with notes of honey, sugarcane, chocolate and caramel.

The roasted coffee travels by DHL Express to Berlin. India qualifies for zero EU import duty under the Developing Countries Trading Scheme, and every shipment carries its proof of origin and our EORI number.

In Berlin it is checked, tasted before it goes anywhere, and packed in 250 g bags with a full German label: product, weight, best-before date, lot number, country of origin, our name and address, and how to store it.

Every bag names where its coffee comes from. That is the point of keeping the route this short.
