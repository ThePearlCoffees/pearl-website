Our Origins · Araku Valley

The coffee grows in the forest.

Araku Valley sits in the Eastern Ghats of Andhra Pradesh, India. This is where Araku Pearl comes from.

The forest

Coffee under the canopy.

The plants grow in the shade of the forest, on highland slopes with mist in the morning. It is a region of tribal farming communities, and coffee is one of the crops they grow.

The 57

57 farmers, six mandals.

57 tribal farmers are onboarded in Araku Valley. We work with them directly, under an MOU with the Araku Tribal Development and Sustainable Agriculture Society.

From cherry to bag

Roasted at origin.

The coffee is roasted in India, close to where it grew, and then shipped to Berlin in 250 g bags.
