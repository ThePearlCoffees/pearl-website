Ch 02 · The hills

Into the Eastern Ghats

Araku Valley sits 1,150 m up, in the hills of Andhra Pradesh. This is where our coffee grows.

Ch 03 · The leaf

Every vein ends at a farmer

Coffee grows here under the forest canopy. Each vein on this leaf stands for one of our 57 farmers.

Ch 04 · The farmers

57 tribal farmers, six mandals

Araku Valley has 21 mandals, which are local districts. Our farmers come from six of them, 35 from the Araku Valley mandal itself. We work with them directly, under a signed MOU with the Araku Tribal Development and Sustainable Agriculture Society.
